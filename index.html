Frequently Asked Questions

Expert answers regarding corporate spend programs in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, and the wider UAE.

Are these corporate cards real credit cards or charge cards?

Most modern corporate card platforms in the UAE (like Alaan, Qashio, Pemo, and Pluto) function primarily as corporate spend cards. They can be backed by prepaid wallets or tied to corporate accounts, allowing zero interest liability. Platforms like Brex and Ramp can also function as corporate charge cards where total spending is cleared at the end of each billing cycle.

What are the eligibility requirements for a corporate card in the UAE?

Usually, providers require an active UAE trade license, a corporate bank account with at least 3 months of bank statements, and identification papers for the authorized manager. Startups backed by venture capital might bypass typical bank statement checks with Brex/Ramp's global venture evaluations.

How do virtual cards manage SaaS subscriptions and advertising?

A virtual card acts exactly like a physical visa/mastercard, but is created instantly on your dashboard. You can issue a dedicated card for Google Ads with a maximum monthly limit of AED 15,000. If that vendor is breached or overcharges, Pluto or Alaan will immediately block further transactions without affecting other active cards.
